datalink-mp captured-game checklist

Everything you need to do by hand for one session of data collection with the traffic capture (branch capture/traffic-capture). It serves three open tickets:

  • internet-play-speed 04, Play a captured internet game: what makes a Turn sync slow (sections B and E).
  • post-0.1.0-polish 05, Does SMAC mark a started game closed to new players? This happens on its own when you start the game in B; there's nothing extra to do.
  • game-session-sync 05, Tell the game when a friend's connection drops: two reproductions, after the speed game (sections C and D).

Mark each step Pass (done, as expected), Fail (didn't work) or Skip. Write down clock times wherever asked: the capture runs on its own clock and needs your times to find the moments that matter. Everything saves in this browser automatically, and Copy results produces Markdown to paste back into the session.
